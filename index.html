How points work: each bowler gets PBA points for their final finish at every event, plus the made-cut bonus and the top-qualifier bonus. Final finish comes from the finals first (champion, runner-up, then by the round they went out in, with ties in a round broken by qualifying position or that round's score), then the 6-game advancers totals, then everyone else by qualifying total. Bowlers tied in qualifying share the same finish and points. Each cell shows points with the finish in grey.
